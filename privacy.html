Verdant: Tower Escape

Privacy notice · last updated 13 September 2026

This is a browser game. It has no accounts of its own, no advertising of its own, and no analytics or tracking scripts. What it does store is listed here in full.

On your device

Your progress — coins, weapon levels, perks, best wave, settings and which tutorial steps you have seen — is saved in your browser's storage, or through the host platform's save system when you play on a portal such as CrazyGames. It never leaves your device unless you submit a score. Clearing your browser data erases it.

If you submit a score

Submitting is optional: nothing is sent unless you press the submit button on the results screen. When you do, this is sent and stored:

  • the callsign you typed (any name you like — it need not be your own),
  • your score: floors or waves reached, kills, run time,
  • the time of submission.

The server also stores a salted one-way hash of your IP address so one person cannot flood the board. The address itself is never written down, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.

Scores are held in a Supabase database and served by a function on Vercel. They are public by design — that is what a leaderboard is.

What is never collected

No email, no real name, no location, no device fingerprint, no advertising identifier, no cross-site tracking, and no cookies of any kind.

Playing on a portal

When the game runs on a portal, that portal's own privacy policy covers their page, their accounts and any advertising they show. This notice covers only what the game itself does.

Removing a score

Ask and it will be deleted. Open an issue at the project's issue tracker with the callsign and roughly when you played.
